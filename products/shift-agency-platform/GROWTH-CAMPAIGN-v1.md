# SHIFT — Growth Engine Campaign Pack v1
**Owner:** Noetfield Systems Inc. | **Start:** 2026-11-09 | **Status:** Ready-to-publish draft assets; nothing yet posted or purchased.

## Differentiated acquisition hook
**Lead magnet:** a usable 10-hook + 3-ad + 7-day agency launch kit *before* requiring an email address.
**Message:** "Don't learn AI. Build with it. Get paid for it." All marketing must make clear that earning money requires real client demand, responsible implementation and selling, not passive income or any assured result.
**Use-case specificity:** home services, restaurants, real estate, ecommerce, fitness, professional firms and creators.

### 12 short-form video drafts

1. **"I built an AI agency launch kit in 60 seconds."** [0–2s] Show the real SHIFT tool. [3–8s] Select a trade niche. [9–18s] Reveal a practical offer and 3 ad angles. [19–27s] Show outreach + execution steps. [28–35s] CTA: "Try the free tool—no account required."
2. **"The skill nobody tells you about AI agencies."** Show the difference between one model prompt and a real buyer problem, handoff, QA and measurement. CTA to agency offer builder.
3. **"Your first offer needs four things."** Buyer / painful workflow / deliverable / measurement, screen-record all four fields. CTA: free offer builder.
4. **"Stop selling 'AI automation' as a vague service."** Rewrite a generic pitch into a specific scheduling follow-up pilot. CTA: generate your niche version.
5. **"This is what a marketing AI agent should NOT do."** Avoid fabricated testimonials, spam and unchecked spend. CTA: responsible campaign drafts.
6. **"7 days from idea to real buyer conversations."** Display daily tasks, clearly state no guaranteed clients. CTA: generate a launch plan.
7. **"Three hooks for a local roofing company."** Show clear educational and problem-led copy. CTA: make your own.
8. **"A restaurant marketing workflow you can actually measure."** Present opt-in rebooking and qualified reservations, never claim revenue without evidence.
9. **"I asked an agency tool to build a B2B offer."** Use real product screen, read actual output, then manually critique and improve it.
10. **"What I would track instead of 'viral' views."** Impressions → clicks → tool activations → accounts → qualified leads → paying customers, with real denominators.
11. **"A cold email nobody should send."** Compare generic bulk spam against specific legally permitted outreach with opt-out requirements.
12. **"I built a free launch tool; here's what it gets wrong."** Publish real limitations openly, invite founder feedback and showcase iterations.

## First 30-day distribution loop

- Week 1: Publish 8 organic demos, 3 educational posts, collect feedback from 10 genuine users, record UTM source and activation. Fix top drop-off.
- Week 2: Produce 8 variant videos based on verified watch data, ship 2 genuine industry-specific landing pages, interview another 10 prospects and approach 5 creator partners with no undisclosed sponsorship.
- Week 3: Publish 8 demos plus one real case study *only with client permission and observed facts*; retarget only where privacy consent and platform rules allow; run one capped paid experiment after explicit budget approval.
- Week 4: Publish 6 strongest angles, evaluate visitors→activation→signup→enquiry→paid, identify actual CAC and creative winners, then refresh all weak-performing landing copy.

**Do not automate spend or messages before audience, budget, accounts, compliance and approvals are configured.**

## Tracking and measurement contract

Real events already supported by SHIFT: `page_view`, `generation`, `signup`, `kit_saved`, `enquiry`, `campaign_csv_export`, `metrics_saved`. UTMs: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`.

Important limitations: events are not yet a fraud-resistant analytics warehouse or ad network conversion pixel, and on-site page views != genuine social impressions. Real account creations are in PostgreSQL. Campaign KPI submissions are manually entered by the signed-in user; never relabel them as verified.

### Growth math — scenarios, not predictions

- 1M social impressions and **1% outbound CTR** produce ~10,000 sessions before drop-off.
- At **10% visitor→account registration**, that's ~1,000 users. The total impression→account rate is **0.1%**.
- At **3% paid conversion** from 1,000 registered users, that's ~30 paying customers, not 1,000.
- To support 1,000 paying subscribers at 3% registration→paid conversion requires ~33,334 signups, and far more top-of-funnel traffic.

The above are sensitivity assumptions to measure, not current industry benchmarks. Target metrics must be monitored via event and billing data.
